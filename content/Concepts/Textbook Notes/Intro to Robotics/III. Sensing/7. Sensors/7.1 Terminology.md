- "Active" sensors emit energy and measure reaction from the environment
- "passive" sensors simply measure energy.

- Range is the difference between and upper and lower limit of the sensor
- Dynamic range is instead the ratio between the upper and lower limit.
- Resolution is the minimal distance between two values

- accuracy is the difference between the average output and the true value
- precision is the ratio of range and statistical variance in a signal


- proprioception is the perception of internal state
- exteroception is sensing anything outside the robot
