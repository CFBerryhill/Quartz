- Force/Torque sensor (or F/T sensor) can detect one or more of a 6 dimensional wrench applied to it
	- costs a lot
	- pretty big (size of a human wrist)
	- low signal to noise ration
	- low bandwidth
	- spares
- Pressure sensors appx. human "touch" using a capacitor (since capacitence is reliant on the distance between plates)
	- Low dimensional compared to F/T sensors
	- High bandwidth
	- high density of sensing
	- easily miniaturized
