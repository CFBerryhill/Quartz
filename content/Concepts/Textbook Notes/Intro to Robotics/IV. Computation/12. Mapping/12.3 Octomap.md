For obstacles, the most common map is an *occupancy grid map*. The environment is discretized into voxels (of arbitrary size) that are either free or have an obstacle. This is imprecise - a slightly better solution is to mark each voxel with the probability it has an obstacle. You can store this information efficiently in a [K-DTree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-d_tree) . Then you do some absolute thresholding to determine if the voxel is free or not.


Highly efficient for planning, but bad at representing small objects.
