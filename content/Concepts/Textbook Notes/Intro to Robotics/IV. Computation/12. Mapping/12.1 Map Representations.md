In order to represent the environment digitally we have two options. 
1. *Discrete Maps* - Tile based map (or Voxel based) with squares or hexagons
2. *Topological maps* or *Graph-based maps* -  every region of the map corresponds to a vertex (also known as a “node”), which are connected by edges if a robot can navigate from one vertex to the other.

Discrete maps are dominant in robotics, but neither is the overall correct choice. Domain matters a lot.
