Statics deals with relating the forces at the robots joints when the robot is in static (mechanical) equilibrium - i.e. the acceleration of the robot and all its components are zero. 

Say we are operating in 6 DoF space ($m=6$)

$$f=\begin{bmatrix}f_{x}\\ f_{y}\\ f_{z}\end{bmatrix}$$ describes the forces on the three axes
$$\mu=\begin{bmatrix}\mu_{x}\\ \mu_{y}\\ \mu_{z}\end{bmatrix}$$
describes the angular force component on the three axes
$$F=\begin{bmatrix}f & \mu\end{bmatrix}^T$$
describes the spatial force or Wrench



Now we would like an equation that relates our $n$x1 vector of torques $\tau$ to F.

Mechanical power is $W=F^{T} \cdot V$ (Force * Velocity)

We know our motors are the source of Power. Therefore

$$W = \tau^{T} \cdot \dot{q} = F^{T} \cdot V$$$$tau^{T} \cdot \dot{q} = F^{T} \cdot J(q) \cdot \dot{q}$$
$$\tau = J(q)^{T} \cdot F$$
There we go!

This essentially say that to counteract an external wrench $F_{e}= -F$, the robot applies torques $\tau$ at its joints.
