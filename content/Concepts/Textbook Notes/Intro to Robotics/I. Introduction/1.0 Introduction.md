The Unimate was the first commercial robot (1961). It opened and poured beer, put a golf ball in a hole, and conducted an orchestra - but this was all preprogrammed. 

Haven't gotten much further - only recently because of a surge of capabilities and sensing have we been making strides. Still very far from human-like performance. 

This book talks a lot about the design and control of autonomous robots. **Robots are considered autonomous when they make decisions in response to their environment.**

The ability to do this hinges heavily on mechanics, sensors, and actuators.
