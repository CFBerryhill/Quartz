Similar data structure to roadmaps designed by humans where you start by planning your path along highways and then work out the details.

> **Definition- Roadmap:** A union of one-dimensional curves is a roadmapp RM iff for all $q_{start}$ and $q_{goal}$ in $Q_{free}$ that can be connected by a path, the following properties hold.
> 	1. ***Accessibility:*** there exists a path from $q_{start} \in Q_{free}$ to some $q^{'}_{start} \in RM$ 
> 	2. ***Departability:*** there exists a path from some $q^{'}_{goal} \in RM$ to $q_{goal} \in Q_{free}$
> 	3. ***Connectivity:*** there exists a path in $RM$ between $q^{'}_{start}$ and $q^{'}_{goal}$. 

5 types of roadmaps: 
- Visibility maps
- deformation retracts
- retract-like structures
- piecewise retracts
- silhouettes
